# Available Animations Reference

This document lists all available animations in the LuxoPi system by category.

## Idle Animations (16)
Looping, energy-conserving animations for idle states.

- `attentive_listening` - Alert and ready, head tilted slightly
- `breathing` - Gentle breathing motion
- `contented_sigh` - Relaxed, satisfied movements
- `curious_exploration` - Looking around with interest
- `dreamy_drift` - Slow, floating movements
- `gentle_sway` - Smooth side-to-side motion
- `head_bobbing` - Rhythmic nodding motion
- `look_around_casual` - Relaxed scanning
- `neck_stretch` - Stretching movements
- `playful_bob` - Light, bouncy movements
- `pondering` - Thoughtful, contemplative pose
- `scanning_watch` - Alert watching behavior
- `settling_adjust` - Small positional adjustments
- `shoulder_shimmy` - Playful shimmy motion
- `tail_wag` - Wagging motion (metaphorical tail)
- `yawning_stretch` - Tired, stretching motion

## Action Animations (6)
Quick, energetic animations for active responses.

- `big_bounce` - Large, dramatic bouncing with explosive movements
- `curious` - Inquisitive, tilting movements
- `dance` - Rhythmic, joyful dance movements
- `small_bounces` - Rapid, continuous small bounces (VERY FAST)
- `stretch` - Full stretching motion
- `think` - Thoughtful pondering motion

## Emotion Animations (2)
Expressive animations for emotional reactions.

- `sad` - Drooping, dejected movements
- `startled` - Quick, surprised reaction

## Response Animations (6)
Clear communicative gestures.

- `big_bow` - Deep, formal bow
- `close` - Bow/curtsy motion (HEAD-ONLY, preserves base)
- `nod` - Affirmative head nod
- `shake` - Negative head shake
- `simple_bow` - Quick, polite bow
- `stop` - Stop/halt gesture

## Petting Animations (3)
Responses to touch interactions.

- `bouncy_wiggle` - Energetic wiggling response
- `folded_wiggle` - Wiggle while folded
- `sleepy_melt` - Relaxed melting motion

## Special States (2)
Special behavioral states.

- `idle` - Basic idle position
- `sleep` - Sleep mode animation

---

## Total: 35 animations

**Note**: Some animations marked as "HEAD-ONLY" preserve the base position and only move upper joints. These are ideal for responses during voice following or other base-dependent behaviors.

**Performance Tip**: Animations marked "VERY FAST" like `small_bounces` are designed for quick, energetic movements and work well with faster speed multipliers.
